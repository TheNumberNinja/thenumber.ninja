# Home page (and the site-wide world it sets)

**Mode:** Persuade. **Scope:** the home page; the Service businesses page is built alongside it as the first inner page, proving the world across page types.

**Audience and job:** a director recommended by someone in Emma's network, checking the practice out between jobs, on desktop or phone. Within seconds they should find their own situation, see whether we fit (including who we're not for), see a real price, and know the next step.

**Action:** Get started (form, then booking calendar). On the home page it sits in the header and closes every answer.

**Proof on hand:** real prices (shared rule section 9), Emma's photo (placeholder until the new shoot), one testimonial (Penni Pickering, Kabo Creative), since 2016, IAB member, Xero partner.

**Constraints:** no illustration budget; no fake textures; no rounded card grids; familiar link and button behaviour; no layout shift; one sans; warmth comes from Emma and the copy. The owner rejected cobalt as a default colour and the signage direction as too governmental.

**Memorable moment:** a visitor reads their own question in large type, opens it, and gets a straight answer with a price, with Emma beside the list as the person answering.

**Unresolved:** final photography (brief the shoot to this palette); the logo (open); Stuart's surname (About page, not needed here).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is built from the questions directors bring to an accountant, in their own words, each answered plainly by the practice with Emma as the person answering. It refuses the category arrangement of a headline, a stock or headshot hero and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Emma's colours, taken from her photo: deep teal field (#0d5c58) carrying whole regions, chartreuse (#cfdc4f) reserved for the opened question, the primary action and the closing bands (plus the current-page, selection and focus marks), pale copper (#f8dccb) for the "not right for you" field; ink (#15172b) and white; nothing else, no gradients, and no tints beyond the derived text and rule tones and the primary button's hover. One sans (Albert Sans, self-hosted), questions set large and semibold, answers at calm reading size. Square edges, hairline rules between questions, drawn arrows. Flat, airy, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees their own question, opens it, reads a straight answer with a real figure, sees who isn't a fit as clearly as who is, learns they'd deal with Emma, and clicks Get started.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal field edge to edge. Header in white with Get started in chartreuse at right. Left nine columns: one sentence saying what The Number Ninja does (about 30px), then "Start with the question you came with" and six questions at about 40px, one per row with rules; "What does it cost?" open on chartreuse showing the £245 price and next-step links. Right three columns: Emma's photo, portrait crop, with a short caption naming her as the person answering.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 ("Your question, answered"), assigned by the roll; seed:704130dfc50804a4. Raises kept from declined challengers: one reserved highlight colour; three colours plus ink and white; "not right for you" designed with the same care as "most of our clients"; one fact per section at poster scale; only the opened question moves; every answer ends with one next step and a way back. Signature interaction: open a question in place (disclosure, one open at a time, height eased open, reduced-motion respected). Code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
